# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary users are support agents and engineering leads working from a shared operations inbox. They use the product in a live support context where the next action matters more than reporting. Operators need to move from a customer message to a safe reply or a structured internal issue without losing evidence.

## Product Purpose

Mend turns WhatsApp conversations into operational work: it receives and answers messages, lets AI triage safe requests, creates native issues, and gives engineering a controlled Codex workbench. Success means fewer dropped requests, better context in issues, and faster, reviewable handoff from support to engineering.

## Positioning

The conversation is the source of truth; every issue, AI suggestion, and engineering run stays connected to it.

## Brand Personality

Focused, precise, quietly confident. The product should feel like an experienced operator's console: dense enough for real work, calm enough for high-pressure moments, and explicit about what is automated versus human-reviewed.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, metric-heavy home screens, chat clones, decorative gradients, green-heavy WhatsApp branding, four-column kanban layouts, and external ticket trackers masquerading as native workflow.

## Design Principles

- Make the conversation the primary object and the issue its structured consequence.
- Show operational state directly: who owns the next action, whether AI is involved, and what changed.
- Keep automation reversible and human-reviewable.
- Prefer dense, calm surfaces over decorative metrics or empty space.
- Treat security boundaries as part of the interface, not invisible infrastructure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use keyboard-visible focus, semantic controls, strong text contrast, reduced-motion support, and labels that do not rely on color alone. The primary workflow must remain understandable at narrow widths and without relying on hover.
